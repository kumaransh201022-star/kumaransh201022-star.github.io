Frequently Asked Questions

How does RentSphere protect owners and renters?

RentSphere is designed around verified users, security deposits, and clear handover rules so both sides know exactly how each rental works.

Why would someone rent instead of buy?

Because many products are needed for a short time only. Renting gives access without a large upfront cost and keeps valuable products in use.

Can students and small owners list items too?

Yes. In the real product idea, anyone with a useful product can list it and earn from the days it is not being used.
